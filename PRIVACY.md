# Kaomoji privacy policy

*Last updated: September 28, 2026*

Kaomoji doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't ask for any permissions.

Everything you do in the app (your favorites, recently used kaomoji, kaomoji you add, and your theme setting) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. If you use Android's device backup, it may include this data; that backup is handled by Android under your Google account.

Kaomoji checks for new versions through Google Play's in-app update service, which is covered by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Questions about this policy: [open an issue](https://github.com/paymonr/kaomoji-releases/issues).
